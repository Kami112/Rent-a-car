How to get leads into this page
1. Run python3 lead_finder.py --niche "car rental" --city Riyadh (needs a Google Places API key, see README.md), then click Import CSV.
2. Or search Google Maps, Instagram, Snapchat and Haraj/Dubizzle yourself and click + Add lead.
3. Click 💬 to open WhatsApp with a personalised message. You press send yourself. Contact at most 20–30 new businesses a day and stop if someone isn't interested, so your number is not reported as spam.
