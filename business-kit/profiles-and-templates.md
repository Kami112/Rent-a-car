## 5. Service packages (starting points, adjust to your market)

Check what similar freelancers charge on Mostaql and Khamsat, then price yourself in that range. Starting low is fine for your first 3–5 reviews.

| Package | What's included | Suggested start |
|---|---|---|
| **Starter website** | 5 pages, AR/EN, mobile, WhatsApp button, contact form | SAR 1,500–3,000 |
| **Booking website** | Starter + fleet/packages, price calculator, booking requests | SAR 3,000–6,000 |
| **Website + dashboard** | Booking website + admin dashboard (bookings, fleet, customers, revenue) | SAR 6,000–12,000 |
| **Social media (monthly)** | 12 designs, captions in Arabic, scheduling, monthly report | SAR 1,000–2,500 / month |
| **Email marketing** | Template design + campaign setup + report | SAR 500–1,500 per campaign |
| **Care plan (monthly)** | Hosting, updates, small edits, backups | SAR 200–500 / month |

Always sell a **monthly plan** alongside a one-off project. Monthly income is what makes freelancing stable.

## 12. Daily routine (about 1 hour)

1. Add 20 new leads (Google Maps, Instagram, Snapchat, Haraj or Dubizzle), or run `lead_finder.py`.
2. Send 20 personalised WhatsApp messages from the Lead Tracker.
3. Do every follow-up the tracker flags as due.
4. Send 3–5 proposals on Mostaql, Khamsat or Upwork.
5. Post one piece of work on LinkedIn or Instagram (screenshot, before/after, short video).
6. Every Sunday, read the weekly job & client alert report and apply to the best matches.
