Our Story

Driven by Trust, Built for the Road

Since 2014, DriveNow Rentals has helped thousands of travelers and locals get behind the wheel with confidence. What started as a single downtown location has grown into a network of 60+ pick-up points, powered by a simple idea: renting a car should be fast, fair and stress-free.

Every vehicle in our fleet is regularly inspected, professionally cleaned, and backed by 24/7 roadside support — so wherever the road takes you, we've got you covered.

  • Transparent, all-inclusive pricing
  • 500+ regularly serviced vehicles
  • 24/7 customer & roadside support
  • Free cancellation on every booking
