Photo in, size out

A customer photographs a door or window opening with an A4 sheet taped beside it. They drag the corners into place, and the app works out the width and height using the A4 sheet as a ruler. Try it on the phone. The sample photo is already loaded.

How the real app would work. This demo finds the door frame and the A4 sheet automatically from their edges, then scales from the sheet. It's accurate to about ±1 to 2 cm when the photo is taken straight on. To make a dot exact, tap it and use the arrow buttons while the magnifier shows the corner up close. The finished app can use a trained AI model for more reliable detection, perspective correction, and AR depth sensing (ARKit on iPhone, ARCore on Android) so customers don't need a reference sheet. It would be built with Flutter or React Native for iOS and Android.
